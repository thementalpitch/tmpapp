# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Athletes who want a quick way to record mental performance notes before and after training, games, recovery, lifting, food, and imagery work. They are often logging in short windows around a session, so the app needs to feel calm, direct, and easy to resume.

## Product Purpose

The MentalPitch helps athletes build a consistent journaling habit around preparation, reflection, mood, and performance patterns. Success means users can start a session quickly, finish it later when needed, and understand their trends without fighting the interface.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, practical. The product should feel like a quiet performance tool, not a motivational poster or a busy analytics cockpit.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS dashboards, neon glow effects, decorative gradients, cramped forms, emoji-heavy stats, and card grids that make every surface feel the same.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep journaling lightweight enough to do before and after real sessions.
- Use restrained color and spacing to reduce cognitive load.
- Preserve native mobile expectations for back navigation, settings, permissions, and touch targets.
- Treat stats as supporting feedback, not the main product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, clear labels above fields, touch targets around 44 px or larger, reduced decorative motion, and readable layouts on small phones.
